How This Version Plays

Instead of steering every second by hand, you define the launch phases. Low-altitude settings shape max-Q survival, upper-atmosphere values shape apoapsis, and the final circularization settings decide whether periapsis rises above the atmosphere.

Mission Goal
  1. Keep the rocket stable through the dense lower atmosphere.
  2. Cross max-Q without breaking the vehicle aerodynamically.
  3. Build enough apoapsis to exit the atmosphere cleanly.
  4. Burn near apoapsis until periapsis stays above the atmosphere.

Max-Q is the period where dense air and high speed combine. Throttle management matters most there, not at orbital altitude.

Start vertically, then pitch down only a little at a time. A gravity turn is efficient because the rocket slowly trades straight-up climb for sideways orbital speed.
