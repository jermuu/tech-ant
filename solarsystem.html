A prograde burn adds tangential speed and usually lifts the far side of the orbit outward. A retrograde burn removes tangential speed and usually drops the orbit inward toward the Sun.

Orbit Formula
v_circ = sqrt(mu / r) -
Delta = v_probe - v_circ -
Delta-v_burn ≈ a x t_burn -

If the probe is faster than circular speed at the same radius, the opposite side of the orbit rises. If it is slower, the orbit falls inward.

Flight Lesson

Use prograde burn to add tangential speed and reach Mars, Jupiter and the outer planets. Use retrograde burn to drop inward and move faster around the Sun.
